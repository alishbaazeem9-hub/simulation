Time impact breakdown
How the levers contribute to total hours freed per year
How every output is calculated
One step per lever — formula and base-case result
1
Lever 01 — Coordination hours reclaimed
Baseline: 18 hrs/campaign managing WhatsApp groups, Sheets, and brief re-confirmations. Portal structured intake + auto-notifications compresses this to the slider target.
Hours freed / yr = (18 − hrs_after) × campaigns_per_year
Productivity gain % = (18 − hrs_after) / 18 × 100
Base: (18 − 6) × 378 = 4,536 hrs freed / yr · 67% gain per campaign
2
Lever 02 — Mismatch time eliminated
Baseline: 33% of campaigns hit a budget-talent mismatch after outreach. Portal budget firewall catches mismatches at intake before any coordinator time is spent.
Prevented / yr = (campaigns_before × 33%) − (campaigns_after × mismatch%_after)
Rework hours avoided / yr = prevented × rework_hrs_per_mismatch
Base: (300 × 33% − 378 × 8%) × 8 = 552 rework hrs avoided / yr
3
Lever 03 — Campaign capacity unlocked
Shorter cycle = same operator can run more campaigns per year. Capacity multiplier capped at 1.8× to reflect operator quality buffer.
Cycle after = 21 × (1 − compression%)
Capacity mult = min(21 / cycle_after, 1.8)
Campaigns / yr (portal) = (campaigns/month × 12) × adoption% × capacity_mult
Brands served / cycle = min(brands_onboarded, round(6 × 18 / hrs_after))
Base: (25 × 12) × 70% × 1.8 = 378 campaigns / yr · min(22, round(6 × 18 / 6)) = 18 brands / cycle
4
Lever 04 — Payment speed gained
Today: 3–7 day delay from WhatsApp proof + WHT surprises. Escrow wallet + WHT disclosed at intake + auto-release on content approval. Fixed target: <24 hrs.
Campaign-days freed / yr = 4 days × campaigns_per_year
Base: 4 × 378 = 1,512 campaign-days freed / yr · 80% faster (fixed)
5
Lever 05 — Brand GMV uplift enabled (0% LAAM take)
Better execution drives brand GMV. Conservative benchmark: Bareen × Hira Faisal +141% (PKR 2.64M incremental). LAAM takes 0% — value is brand loyalty and retention.
Brand GMV enabled = campaigns/yr × uplift_hit_rate% × avg_incremental_GMV
Base: 378 × 45% × PKR 1,200,000 = PKR 204M brand GMV / yr · LAAM take: PKR 0
