How every number is calculated
Full methodology — every formula, every assumption, every data source
Core logic
PRaaS charges 0% commission. Value is entirely operational: less time per campaign, fewer late mismatches, more brands per cycle, and better-executed campaigns driving brand GMV. All three levers are measured in hours, then converted to PKR via operator salary. Baselines are fixed from real data — only targets and adoption are adjustable.
1
Campaign capacity with portal
Faster cycle = same operator handles more campaigns. Cycle compression set by slider (default 53% = 21 → 9.8 days). Multiplier capped at 1.8× — operator needs quality buffer.
Cycle after = 21 × (1 − compression%)
Capacity mult = min(21 / cycle_after, 1.8)
Campaigns / yr (portal) = (25 × 12) × adoption% × capacity_mult
Base: 300 × 69% × 1.8 = 372 campaigns / year vs 300 today
2
Lever 01 — Total time freed (3 components)
Coordination: Operator log confirms ~18 hrs/campaign managing 25+ WhatsApp groups, updating Sheets, chasing payments. Structured intake + Kanban + auto-notifications compresses this to the slider target.
Coord hrs freed / yr = (18 − hrs_after) × campaigns_yr
Approval rounds: Content currently goes through 3–4 WhatsApp rounds (brand → operator → talent → revise → repeat). Portal's review thread collapses this to 1 structured round. Each round saved = 45 min.
Approval hrs freed / yr = rounds_saved × 0.75 hrs × campaigns_yr
Mismatch rework: 33% of campaigns hit a budget-talent mismatch discovered after hours of outreach. Budget firewall catches this at intake. Each prevented mismatch saves 8 hrs of rework.
Rework avoided / yr = (campaigns_before × 33% − campaigns_after × mismatch%) × 8 hrs
PKR value = total_hrs_freed × (salary ÷ 176) × team_size
Base: (12×372 + 2×0.75×372 + 69×8) = 4,464 + 558 + 552 = ~5,574 hrs / yr · PKR ~2.38M
3
Lever 02 — Capacity unlocked
Constraint is coordination overhead, not talent availability. 18 hrs × 6 brands = 108 operator-hours per cycle. At 6 hrs/campaign the same 108 hours serves 18 brands. Peak season confirms this: 32 brands aligned, only 6 executed — PKR 38.75M in confirmed budgets went unserved.
Brands / cycle = min(brands_onboarded, round(6 × 18 / hrs_after))
Unserved budget = additional_brands × (38.75M ÷ 26)
Base: min(22, round(6×18/6)) = 18 brands / cycle · +PKR 14.9M unserved budget accessible
4
Lever 03 — Brand sales uplift (0% LAAM take)
Better execution (right talent, validated brief, no mismatch, inventory checked) drives brand GMV uplift. LAAM takes 0% — the value for LAAM is brand loyalty and retention. Conservative benchmark: Bareen × Hira Faisal +141% (PKR 2.64M incremental). GMV per campaign fixed at PKR 1.2M.
Brand GMV / yr = campaigns_yr × uplift_hit_rate% × PKR 1,200,000
Base: 372 × 45% × 1.2M = PKR 201M brand GMV enabled · LAAM take: PKR 0
Why Sajal Aly is excluded from the GMV benchmark
Urge Pret × Sajal Aly generated +652% GMV (PKR 2.76M → PKR 20.73M) — a top-0.1% A-tier result that cannot be replicated across 32 brands. The base uses PKR 1.2M incremental GMV, roughly 45% of what Bareen × Hira Faisal achieved, which is genuinely conservative.

Why the Kainat Faisal campaigns failed — and how the portal prevents this
The two Kainat Faisal kaftaan campaigns fell −59% and −91% in March 2026. Kaftaan was already a declining seasonal product in February — this campaign should never have gone live in March. The portal's inventory gate and brief validation layer would have flagged this at submission, before any coordinator time was spent.
