Impact Simulation · LAAM Group (Pvt.) Ltd. · PRaaS Portal — Scenario C
From WhatsApp chaos to structured workflow — how much time does the portal free?
This simulator quantifies the operational impact of replacing LAAM's fragmented WhatsApp + Google Sheets pipeline with a custom PRaaS portal. LAAM charges brands and talents 0% commission — PRaaS is a complementary service. Impact is measured in time saved, coordination eliminated, capacity unlocked, and brand GMV enabled. All baseline numbers are grounded in Aiman Murtaza's operator logs (18 hrs/campaign) and Maliha Noor's mismatch interview (33% rejection rate).
0% Commission Model LAAM charges brands and talents zero commission on PRaaS campaigns. The portal is a complementary service — value is measured in operator time saved, cycle speed, capacity unlocked, and brand GMV enabled, not revenue extraction.

Core logic
LAAM's PRaaS portal charges 0% commission. The value is in operational efficiency: less time per campaign, fewer mismatches caught late, more brands served per cycle, faster payments, and better-executed campaigns generating more GMV for brands. Every lever is calculated from time units. Baseline numbers are fixed from real interviews — only targets and adoption are adjustable.
1
Campaign capacity with portal
Faster cycle time = same operator can handle more campaigns. Cycle compression is set by the slider (default 53% = 21 days → 9.8 days). We cap the capacity multiplier at 1.8× to be conservative — the operator also needs buffer for quality reviews.
Cycle after = 21 × (1 − compression%) · Capacity mult = min(21 / cycle_after, 1.8)
Campaigns / yr (portal) = (campaigns / month × 12) × adoption% × capacity_mult
Base: (25 × 12) × 70% × 1.8 = 378 campaigns / year vs 300 today
2
Lever 01 — Coordination hours reclaimed
Aiman Murtaza confirmed ~18 hrs/campaign: managing 25+ WhatsApp groups, updating Sheets manually, calling brands to re-confirm briefs, chasing payments. Portal's structured intake + Kanban board + auto-notifications compresses this to the slider value (default 6 hrs). Baseline of 18 hrs is fixed from the operator log.
Hours freed / yr = (18 − hrs_after) × campaigns_per_year
Productivity gain % = (18 − hrs_after) / 18 × 100
Base: (18 − 6) × 378 = 4,536 hours freed / year · 67% productivity gain
3
Lever 02 — Mismatch time eliminated
Maliha Noor confirmed 33% of campaigns hit a budget-talent mismatch — discovered after hours of outreach. The portal's budget firewall catches this at the intake form before any time is invested in outreach. The 33% baseline is fixed; the target rate is the slider. Each prevented mismatch saves the rework hours set by the slider.
Prevented / yr = (campaigns_before × 33%) − (campaigns_after × mismatch%_after)
Rework hours avoided / yr = prevented × rework_hrs_per_mismatch
Base: (300 × 33% − 378 × 8%) × 8 = 69 × 8 = 552 rework hours avoided / year
4
Lever 03 — Campaign capacity unlocked
The constraint is not talent availability — it is operator coordination overhead. Currently, 18 hrs/campaign × 6 brands/cycle = 108 operator-hours. With the portal at 6 hrs/campaign, the same 108 hours serves 18 brands. Peak Season 2026 confirms this: 32 brands aligned, only 6 executed. PKR 38.75M in budgets went unserved because of a coordination ceiling.
Brands served / cycle = min(brands_onboarded, round(6 × 18 / hrs_after))
Base: min(22, round(6 × 18 / 6)) = 18 brands per cycle · +12 additional brands
5
Lever 04 — Payment speed gained
Today: 3–7 day payment delay because proof is shared via WhatsApp screenshot and WHT (14% withholding tax) surprises brands mid-campaign. The portal's escrow wallet holds payment proof in-system, WHT is disclosed at New Brief stage, and release triggers automatically on content approval. Target: <24 hours. This is fixed as a target per Step 7 — not a slider — because it is a binary system feature, not a gradual improvement.
Fixed assumption: 5 days before → 1 day after (80% faster) · campaign-days freed = 4 × campaigns/yr
Base: 4 days × 378 campaigns = 1,512 campaign-days freed / year
6
Lever 05 — Brand GMV uplift enabled (0% LAAM take)
The portal enables better-executed campaigns — right talent, validated brief, right timing, no inventory mismatch. Better execution drives brand GMV uplift. LAAM takes 0% of this GMV; the value for LAAM is brand loyalty and platform retention. We use Bareen × Hira Faisal (+141%, PKR 2.64M incremental) as the conservative benchmark.
Brand GMV enabled = campaigns/yr × uplift_hit_rate% × avg_incremental_GMV
Base: 378 × 45% × PKR 1,200,000 = PKR 204M brand GMV enabled / year · LAAM take: PKR 0
Why Sajal Aly is excluded from the base case GMV figure
Urge Pret × Sajal Aly generated +652% GMV (PKR 2.76M → PKR 20.73M). This is a top-0.1% A-tier celebrity result that cannot be replicated across LAAM's full 32-brand portfolio. Using it would inflate projections dishonestly. The base case uses PKR 1.2M incremental GMV — approximately 45% of what Bareen × Hira Faisal actually achieved (PKR 2.64M) — which is genuinely conservative.

Why the Kainat Faisal campaigns failed — and how the portal prevents this
The two Kainat Faisal kaftaan campaigns declined −59% and −91% in March 2026. Kaftaan is a seasonal product already declining in February. The problem: this campaign should never have been approved for March. The portal's inventory gate (blocks go-live if stock is declining) and brief validation layer (checks product seasonality at submission) would have caught this before any coordinator time was spent — preventing a poor result from being attributed to the talent.
